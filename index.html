GOOD TO KNOW

FREQUENT
QUESTIONS.

Not sure where to start? Contact the team for advice based on your vehicle.

What does paint protection film do? +

PPF is a protective film designed to help reduce damage from everyday road debris and minor surface wear. The appropriate coverage depends on the car and usage.

Can I change my car's appearance with wrapping? +

Vinyl wrapping provides an alternative finish or color without a conventional repaint. Ask the workshop about available products, styles and care requirements.

Do you offer custom lighting solutions? +

Custom headlights and lighting modification are among ONEighty's listed services. Options and compatibility should be confirmed for your exact model.

How can I receive a quotation? +

Use the quote form below to prepare a WhatsApp message with your vehicle and requested service, or contact the official Instagram account.

Where is ONEighty GCC located? +

ONEighty is listed in the Tubli / Manama area of Bahrain. Check with the team before visiting for their up-to-date location and working hours.
